Phase 1 · Discovery & Systems Audit

June · Foundational audit of existing instructional systems & frameworks

Upcoming

This foundational phase focuses on understanding Portales Municipal School District's current reality. Rather than starting from scratch, the goal is to identify what is already working and align the new strategic direction with PMSD's existing systems, frameworks, initiatives, and accountability structures.

Key deliverables
PMSD Systems Alignment Mapping Crosswalk
Strategic Systems Audit Report
District Readiness Snapshot

Phase 1 audit questions

Learn New Mexico Administrators Only

Use the questions below to guide PMSD's Phase 1 discovery conversations. Complete all sections before generating a summary report.

A. Instructional systems inventory — identify what already exists
1. What instructional frameworks, curricula, or programs are currently in place across PMSD schools?
Consistency rating
2. Which district-wide initiatives are currently active, and what accountability structures support them?
Implementation depth
3. What professional development systems are in place, and how are they tied to instructional goals?
Alignment strength
B. Alignment & gap analysis — surface misalignment before planning
4. Where do current instructional systems conflict, overlap, or create confusion for teachers?
Severity of gaps
5. Which existing PMSD frameworks or initiatives should be preserved and built upon in the new strategic plan?
6. Are there redundant programs consuming resources without measurable impact? If so, which?
Resource impact
C. District readiness snapshot — assess PMSD's capacity for change
7. How would you describe PMSD leadership's current appetite and capacity for strategic change?
8. What data systems does PMSD currently use to track student outcomes, and how accessible is that data to school leaders?
Data accessibility
9. What previous strategic plans or improvement efforts has PMSD undertaken, and what can be learned from their outcomes?
D. Stakeholder & community readiness — who needs to be engaged first
10. Which stakeholder groups in Portales have the most trust equity with PMSD, and which have historically felt excluded?
11. What community partners currently support PMSD, and how are those partnerships formalized or tracked?
Partnership strength
12. Are there known political or community sensitivities the strategic planning process should navigate carefully?

September · Drafting PMSD's measurable, operational, implementable goals

Upcoming
